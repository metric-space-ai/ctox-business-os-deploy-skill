# Business OS App Design Preflight

Use this fallback whenever `/ctox` connects a coding agent that will create,
modify, review, or repair a Business OS frontend. When the instance advertises
the newer `business-os-app-module-development` resource, read and follow it as
the authoritative superset.

## Preflight

Before mutation, identify:

- the first visible repeated business task;
- the wide, compact, 640×480, 390×844, and 360px layout;
- the scroll owner and pane access/return path at each size;
- routine controls that stay flat, neutral, and compact;
- the zero-or-one domain-named signature AI automation and its typed command,
  approval, queue/progress, result, failure, abort, and retry states;
- records that expose Context v2 right-click identity;
- theme-token behavior in light, dark, custom branding, German, and English.

Emit before implementation:

`IMPECCABLE_PREFLIGHT: context=pass product=pass command_reference=pass shape=pass image_gate=pass mutation=open`

Keep `mutation=blocked` when any required shape or evidence is missing.

## Presentation Contract

- Use root `launch_kind: desktop-app` and the canonical `presentation` object.
- Floating windows resize with real mouse input down to 640×480.
- At a shell width of 600px or below, switch to a mobile app sheet supported
  down to 360px. Keep Start, app name, version/status, Source, Versions/
  Rollback, close/back, chat, and the upper app switcher reachable. Do not add a
  second app switcher beside the chat dock.
- Preserve state across window, maximized, focus, and mobile-sheet changes.

## Operational Density

Use Ableton Live only as a reference for density, immediacy, spatial memory,
and functional color.

- Import, search, filter, sort, select, edit, save, export, navigation, status,
  and window controls are compact and visually quiet.
- At most one real signature AI automation per visible work surface may use
  prominent color or extra space.
- Create, Save, Export, Settings, and generic Ask AI are ordinary controls, not
  hero actions.
- Reject landing-page heroes, metric mosaics, glass/glow, generic AI
  dashboards, nested cards, decorative panes, and duplicated shell chrome.

## Panes and Evidence

- Wide two-/three-pane workbenches use shell-owned resizers.
- Compact/mobile layouts stack panes or provide a visible tab/drawer/back path.
  Never silently hide a context or detail pane.
- Prove Start/Desktop single click, real mouse window and pane resize,
  maximize/restore, minimize/restore through the upper app bar, close, mobile
  pane access, chat coexistence, keyboard focus, Reduced Motion, and long
  app-name labels. Direct manager calls or style mutation do not count as
  resize proof.
